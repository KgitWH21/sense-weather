A quick way to use the seed

Write one physical detail, let the character give it meaning, then make that meaning change an action. A comforting sensation can carry an uncomfortable emotion. The mismatch is part of the invitation.

Perception starts with an observable cue and offers a character's interpretation. You decide whether they're right, mistaken, or sensing something uncanny.

Choose a sense to practice it, or choose All senses for variety. Favor touch gives touch three times the chance in an all-senses roll. Keep any card and reroll the rest.

Phone setup: download the package, publish its contents with GitHub Pages, open the page on your phone, and choose Add to Home screen. It works offline after its first successful load. The README includes setup steps.
